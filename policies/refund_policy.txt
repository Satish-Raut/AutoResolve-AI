# STRIDELIFE CORP. - OFFICIAL REFUND & RETURN POLICY

1. STANDARD RETURN WINDOW
Customers are eligible to return any item purchased from StrideLife within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be in clean, unworn, and resaleable condition. The original packaging must be intact.

2. FULL VS PARTIAL REFUNDS
- Full Refunds: Eligible only if returned within 30 days of delivery, with original tags and unworn status.
- Partial Refunds (80% value): Allowed for items returned between 31 to 45 days of delivery, or items returned slightly used/without original packaging.
- No Refunds: Strictly no cash refunds or credit returns for items past 45 days of delivery under any circumstances.

3. SPECIAL ITEMS & EXCEPTIONS
- Custom/Made-to-Order Shoes: Non-refundable and final sale, unless there is a clear manufacturing defect confirmed by the inspection team.
- Promotional/BOGO items: Refunded at the individual discounted purchase price, not the full retail price.

4. OVERRIDES AND MANUAL DISCRETION
Only Tier 3 Support Managers have the permission to override the 30-day return window. Overrides are limited to cases of verified shipping damage, incorrect product delivery, or system billing errors.

5. RESTOCKING FEES
No restocking fee is charged for standard returns or size exchange requests. A flat $10 shipping and handling fee is deducted for international returns.
