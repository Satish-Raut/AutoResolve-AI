# STRIDELIFE CORP. - PRIVACY, GDPR & DATA SECURITY POLICY

1. DATA DELETION REQUESTS (GDPR / CCPA)
Under GDPR and CCPA regulations, customers have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data (the "Right to be Forgotten").
- Procedure: The customer must submit a written request from their registered email.
- Execution: Our security agents must verify the identity and manually wipe the user record from databases. Wiping takes 7 to 10 business days to complete.
- Audit Trail: A secure hash of the transaction history must be archived for tax compliance, but all identifiable data (name, email, phone, shipping addresses) will be purged.

2. SECURITY VERIFICATION
No support agent is permitted to share tracking details, credit card records, or billing addresses to a user without validating the following:
- The ticket was submitted from the official email address attached to the purchase.
- If submitted from a secondary email, the user must provide the last 4 digits of the card used, the billing zip code, and the order date for verification.

3. ACCOUNT MERGING
We do not support automatic account merging. If a customer has two separate accounts, they must decide which email they want to keep. We will transfer VIP loyalty points manually and deactivate the secondary email.
