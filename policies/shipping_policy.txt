# STRIDELIFE CORP. - OFFICIAL SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY

1. STANDARD DELIVERY TIMELINE
- Standard Domestic Shipping: 3 to 5 business days from order placement.
- Express Domestic Shipping: 1 to 2 business days.
- International Shipping: 7 to 14 business days.

2. DELAY NOTIFICATIONS & COMPENSATION
If an order is delayed past 3 business days of the estimated delivery date due to carrier backlogs or weather:
- Standard shipping fee ($5.99) is fully refunded as an apology credit.
- For express shipping delays, the express premium charge ($14.99) is refunded.

3. LOST IN TRANSIT PROTOCOL
A package is officially declared "Lost in Transit" if there has been no tracking status update for more than 7 consecutive business days.
- Once declared lost, customer is eligible for a full product replacement sent via express shipping at no cost, or a full refund.
- Exception: If the carrier tracking states "Delivered" and has a signature verification, the claim is rejected. The customer must file a police report for package theft before we can issue an escalation.

4. WRONG OR DAMAGED ITEMS
If a customer receives an incorrect size, color, or a physically damaged product, StrideLife will immediately issue a prepaid return shipping label and ship the correct replacement item immediately at zero additional charge.
