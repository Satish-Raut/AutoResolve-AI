# STRIDELIFE CORP. - VIP CLUB & SUBSCRIPTION MEMBERSHIP POLICY

1. MEMBERSHIP PLANS
- StrideLife VIP Club: $19.99 per month. Offers free shipping, 20% off all shoes, and early access to drops.

2. CANCELLATION RULES
Members can cancel their VIP Club subscription at any time through their online dashboard. Upon cancellation:
- VIP benefits remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.
- The subscription will not auto-renew for the next month.

3. SUBSCRIPTION REFUNDS
- standard 14-Day Cooling-off Period: New subscribers are eligible for a full refund of their first month's fee ($19.99) if they cancel and request a refund within 14 days of subscribing, provided they have not placed any orders using VIP benefits.
- Post-14 Days: No refunds are issued for mid-cycle cancellations. Fees are non-refundable.

4. TRIAL EXTENSIONS
Tier 1 and Tier 2 Support Agents are authorized to offer a one-time 15-day free trial extension to disgruntled customers who threaten to cancel their subscription due to financial constraints or poor product options.
